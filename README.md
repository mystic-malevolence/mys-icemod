
Ice Mod
=======

This mod adds ice bricks and cracked ice bricks. More ice and snow themed building blocks may be added in the future.
I created this in the course of learning how to create mods for Minecraft.

The subject was chosen because I occasionally, tragically lack ice bricks in some mod packs,
and even when I do have access to them I am often underwhelmed by their art direction. The ice bricks here are of a unique shape not taken from any existing bricks.
